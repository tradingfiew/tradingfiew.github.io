How TradingFiew Analysis Works

Updated 16 November 2026

TradingFiew is a free educational charting tool for gold, Nasdaq 100 reference, S&P 500 and Dow Jones references, forex, energy, aggregated share prices and Binance Spot crypto. TradingFiew is not an order execution venue and does not provide investment advice.

Market data

Binance Spot cryptocurrency prices come from Binance public market feeds. Non-crypto market prices originate from biquote's indicative broker-derived MetaTrader 5 or aggregated feeds; they may be delayed, stale, and different from official exchange prints or your broker's tradeable prices. Watch for closed-market conditions and verify prices before making any decision.

Structure: BOS and CHoCH

Swing pivots require neighboring closed candles to confirm. Breaking a confirmed swing with a candle close is classified using the previous structure direction: BOS for a continuation, CHoCH for an opposing break. They are historical heuristics rather than proof of institutional order flow.

FVG, order blocks and liquidity

Fair Value Gaps are three-candle price gaps between the first and third candle's extremities. Candidate order blocks are located using the last opposite-direction candle before a structure break. Liquidity sweep labels identify a wick beyond a confirmed pivot followed by a close back within its level. These definitions are simplified and may differ from other trading platforms.

Price line and support/resistance

The green LAST line displays the most recently available quote. S1 and R1 identify confirmed pivot-low and pivot-high areas around price, with nearby swing observations clustered using an ATR tolerance. No support or resistance level is a guaranteed boundary or an order recommendation.

Other indicators

EMA 20/50/200, RSI 14, ATR 14 and higher-timeframe market context are provided for technical study. The displayed confluence count is the number of matching criteria, not a calibrated probability or trading signal. Trading financial markets can lead to substantial losses.
