FREE MARKET RESEARCH

Free gold, Nasdaq, forex & crypto charts with Smart Money Concepts

TradingFiew is a browser-based charting tool for people researching price action. Follow XAUUSD gold charts, Nasdaq 100 indicative charts, EUR/USD forex charts, and Bitcoin charts. Choose 5-minute, 15-minute, hourly, four-hour or daily candles and explore market structure with multiple indicators.

Smart Money Concepts analysis

Explore Break of Structure (BOS), Change of Character (CHoCH), three-candle Fair Value Gaps (FVG), candidate order blocks and liquidity sweeps based on confirmed swing points. These labels are technical heuristics, not signals from institutional trading desks.

Support, resistance & indicators

Identify confirmed pivot-based support and resistance around price, with an adjustable chart view, EMA 20/50/200, RSI 14, ATR 14, volume, drawing tools and a higher-timeframe structure check.

Sources and market-hours transparency

Cryptocurrency Spot candles use Binance's public feed. Gold, Nasdaq, indices, commodities, forex and equity references use indicative or aggregated data that may be stale or differ from official exchange or broker executable quotes.

TERMINAL GUIDE

Read the chart. Don't chase the chart.

Choose a market and timeframe. The SMC engine works on confirmed swing pivots, labeling structure breaks (BOS), changes of character (CHoCH), three-candle Fair Value Gaps, order block candidates and liquidity sweeps.

Set a phone or computer price alert directly on the chart: tap the left-side 🔔 tool, then tap any price level on the live chart. An alert is saved immediately with a thin amber dotted line; tap the Cursor tool to finish. Desktop users can also right-click anywhere inside the chart. Open the top 🔔 Alerts manager to pause, resume, or delete alerts, to test the sound, or to grant phone notifications. Notifications only run while TradingFiew is running (a separate push backend would be needed after closing it).

Chart + RSI: drag, zoom or move the crosshair in either panel. Both charts track the same time and show the same vertical cursor. Each EMA, SMC zone color, level and alert guide can be shown or hidden independently from Indicators. The current-price line displays the quote and candle countdown. Moving your pointer over the chart draws a dashed horizontal crosshair with the hovered price. Drag any user-drawn line or alert line to adjust it.

Draw: pick Horizontal, Trend or Fib from the chart toolbar, then click one or two chart points. Click the cursor tool to stop drawing. Tap any custom drawing to select it and press Delete, or open Manage Drawings (☷) to delete an individual line or Fibonacci level. Use the indicators menu for EMA, volume, RSI and overlays.

Prices: historical crypto candles use Binance Spot data; the prices and WebSocket updates. Gold, indices, forex and stocks use indicative broker or aggregated feeds. The market can be closed or stale. No simulated prices.

Important: patterns are historical heuristics and are not verified institutional order flow, investment advice, or a guarantee of profit.
