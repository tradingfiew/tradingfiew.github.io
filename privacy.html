Privacy Policy

Updated 16 November 2026

Purpose

TradingFiew is an independent free chart research website, with no account registration, wallet custody, or order placement.

On-device settings

Chart analysis is performed in the browser. Favorite markets, preferred symbol, timeframe, technical indicators, the chart window setting, theme, manually drawn price levels, trend lines and Fibonacci levels are saved in the localStorage of your browser profile. TradingFiew uses an anonymous locally generated workspace ID; this is not a user account or verified identity. Anyone with access to the same browser profile can see its saved preferences and drawings. Separate browser profiles or devices use separate storage and are not automatically synchronized. Private browsing sessions and blocked storage can lose changes when closed. You can erase the workspace in the My Space panel or remove it using your browser's website-data controls.

Saved market alerts and phone notifications

TradingFiew optionally stores alert rules, chosen symbols and prices, enabled status, repeat and sound settings, notification preferences and recently triggered events in the local storage of your browser workspace. You can pause, resume, delete alerts, clear recent events or erase the local workspace using My Space. Alert rules are not synchronized across your devices and are not uploaded to a TradingFiew account.

To check alert rules for other markets while the website remains open, your browser may make periodic requests to the public Binance Spot and indicative biquote market-data services. Browser/phone notifications require your explicit permission. A service worker may display a notification and open the relevant market when you tap it. This static website has no remote push server or push subscription service: it cannot reliably monitor markets or send new alerts after all TradingFiew pages are closed. Browsers may throttle or suspend pages in the background, and device settings can disable sound or notifications.

SMC breakout scenarios are disabled by default and are technical heuristics based on confirmed closing candles. They are not personalized investment recommendations, executable orders or guarantees. Individual decisions and trading risks remain yours.

External market data

Your browser contacts Binance for Spot cryptocurrency market data, and biquote for indicative gold, forex, index, energy and aggregated equity data. These suppliers and the hosting platform may receive technical information such as IP address, request timestamps and user agent as needed to serve their services. The site also loads font assets and TradingView Lightweight Charts from third-party CDNs.

Advertising

Google AdSense ownership verification and ads.txt are present. Display advertising has not been activated on the website pending separate AdSense site approval and appropriate consent management configuration. If advertising starts later, this notice must be updated beforehand to disclose cookies, personalized or non-personalized advertising and consent choices.

Donation feature and optional public supporter credit

The website shows a voluntarily provided USDT TRC-20 receiving address on the TRON network. BTC, ETH and USDT ERC-20 options are unavailable until separately configured. Transfers are ordinarily irreversible; verify the coin, network and receiving address before donating. TradingFiew does not process wallet custody, record private keys or automatically identify donors.

Donations are anonymous by default. If a donor separately chooses to request public recognition, they enter a display name or pseudonym and check an initially unchecked consent box. That voluntary action opens an optional public GitHub Issue, which requires a GitHub account and may display the submitter's GitHub identity. No display name is published to the TradingFiew supporters wall until the project owner confirms the donation and explicitly opts that name in to the public list. We do not show donors' wallet addresses, amounts or transaction IDs in the supporters wall.

A site visit, opening the Donate panel or copying the receiving address does not submit personal data or consent to public recognition. The public supporters list is stored in a static public file and removal requests should be made through the project's contact or GitHub Issues page.

Reserved advertising areas

The terminal displays labeled advertising spaces as placeholders. These are not paid ads, and no AdSense ad requests are initiated until the site has obtained approval and required consent management has been configured.

Questions

For project feedback you can use the public GitHub Issues page. Do not post private information there.
