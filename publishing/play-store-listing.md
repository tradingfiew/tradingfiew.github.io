## Full description (English)
Explore market price action using TradingFiew, an independent technical chart and Smart Money Concepts research tool.

• Interactive candlestick charts for gold, forex, indices and Binance Spot crypto.
• Multiple timeframes, EMA 20/50/200, RSI and support/resistance.
• BOS, CHoCH, Fair Value Gap (FVG) and liquidity sweep overlays.
• Draw your own price levels, trend lines and Fibonacci retracements.
• Set optional local price alerts, customize their sound and notifications.
• Save your preferences and drawings on your device without an account.

Data availability and freshness vary. Crypto Spot data comes from Binance public feeds. Other quotes can be indicative, delayed or stale and are not executable prices.

Important: TradingFiew provides educational market research, not financial advice or trade execution. Alerts depend on the app being active and data services being reachable. Guaranteed alerts after the app is closed are not offered.

TradingFiew is not affiliated with or endorsed by TradingView or any exchange.
